# excalidraw-drawing changelog

## 2.0.0 (2026-11-06)

- Renamed from `sketch-infographic` to `excalidraw-drawing`: since 1.1.0 it draws charts and diagrams as well as infographic pages, and the old name described only the pages. The new one names what every picture is made in, so it stays true as more kinds are added. A host or prompt that calls `sketch-infographic` must use the new name; the skill, its docs, its test (`tests/excalidraw-drawing-test`) and its install links all moved, and the runtime override is now `EXCALIDRAW_DRAWING_RUNTIME`. Releases up to 1.1.0 are tagged `sketch-infographic/v<version>`; from 2.0.0, `excalidraw-drawing/v<version>`. On a computer that installed the old name, bootstrap removes the old links (`retired` rows in `install/links.tsv`, a new mode: a link is removed only while it points at this checkout's old folder; a link to a `skills/sketch-infographic` folder elsewhere, from another or a moved checkout, is kept and reported for the owner to remove, and anything else at the path is kept), uninstall removes them too, and doctor fails while one of this checkout's is left and warns about one from another checkout. From the review (Deep, two rounds): the first version of the rename left the old links dangling on every other computer; removing any broken link of the right shape could delete one this repository never installed. The `.excalidraw` files it writes now give `excalidraw-drawing` as their source; nothing else about what the skill draws changed.

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-06)

- Draws single diagrams as well as infographic pages: the description and the workflow now cover a line or bar chart, flowchart, boundary, sequence diagram, timeline, quadrant, cycle, tree or comparison table, planned from the user's numbers, description or example image. From redoing three kinds of example image as a test (a line chart, a flow diagram and a sequence diagram): all three built, but only by tracing points by eye, passing an undocumented Excalidraw field through, and fighting the page frame, and a request for "a hand-drawn chart" might not have loaded the skill at all.
- New `references/diagrams.md`: a table for picking a diagram by its point, then one tested full-width snippet per kind with what to watch for. The test builds every one, so a snippet can't silently break.
- New `chart` element: a line chart (series of points, axes with optional ticks, grid and markers, each series named at its first or last point) or a bar chart, scaled from the numbers, so no point or bar length is worked out by hand. A point or tick outside its axis, a squeezed plot, a series of fewer than two points or a negative bar is a warning. The patterns' bar chart uses it instead of hand-scaled rectangles.
- `"curve": true` on a line or arrow draws a smooth curve through its points; `"frame": false` on the page or a panel drops the panel's card and padding; `"format": "fit"` also trims the page's width to the drawing. Arrow labels are documented as drawn over by their line in the PNG, with a text beside the arrow as the alternative, after the first flowchart in the catalog had "yes" and "no" struck through.
- Reviewed at Standard (Codex GPT-6.1 Terra; Claude Haiku 5.5 on trial). Fixed from it, each with a test: an all-zero line series was dropped, a start label sat by the axis even when its series started further right, a zero bar looked like a small one, charts with y ticks drew curves that could bulge past their measurements (now straight by default), series names pushed apart could leave the chart's box, and the catalog test skipped a diagram whose snippet was missing.
- A gallery of every catalog diagram, rendered, in `docs/skills/sketch-infographic/gallery/` (since 2.0.0, `docs/skills/excalidraw-drawing/gallery/`), for the owner to pick from; agents never load it.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-05)

- First release: plan a page from one source (title, five to seven panels, footer), write it as a JSON spec, and build it into a PNG and an editable `.excalidraw` file. The build lays out a two-column grid, wraps text, routes arrows, and warns about elements outside their panel, overlaps, boxes that grew for their label, and characters Excalifont lacks. From recreating a hand-drawn infographic seen on X as a private test: the first build showed that typed arrows and check marks fall back to a system font, that the renderer centres a label it was asked to put at the top, and that an overlap check skipping text inside boxes misses the collision that results.
- Pages have a fixed shape, 3:4 portrait (1500×2000) by default, with 4:5, A4, square and auto. The plan fills the page with six or seven panels, keeps every panel of an example page, and the check compares against it side by side. Type sizes, margins and panel padding are measured from two 1500×2000 example pages. From the owner's review of the first test page: it came out 1500×1670 against the example's 1500×2000, with a row of panels missing and larger type.
- Takeaways sit right under the drawing instead of at the panel's bottom, so a stretched panel keeps its words with its picture; left-aligned box labels, hex stroke colors, sheets without "•••" and stacks fanning up and to the right work; empty text is skipped instead of crashing Excalidraw's restore.
- A `group` field keeps a drawing's parts together, and the build warns when an arrow or line runs through text. From recreating a second example page: an arrow crossed a caption without a warning, and a cylinder built from two shapes was reported as an overlap. That page first came out 207 px too tall for 3:4; the overflow warning's number was enough to tighten it to fit in two rounds.
- Arrows sharing a side of a box fan out along it, arrows to an ellipse end on its curve, and an arrow under 20 px is a warning; the visual check asks whether every arrow starts and ends on something. From the owner's review of the second recreation: three arrowheads stacked on one point of a circle, a dashed line started in empty space, and two pairs of boxes left arrows of 8 to 12 px.
- Reviewed at Deep before release, over three rounds (Codex GPT-6.1 Terra and Claude Sonnet 5.5; GPT-6 Luna on trial; both free reviewers ran out of time). Nine must-fixes, each fixed with a test: labels overflowing their box without a warning, text over text, a crash on a stack without an id or a panel without a heading or title, a PNG and an .excalidraw file left from different builds after a failed render or write, a flag without a value ignored, words too long for their text, and doctor checking the runtime for hosts that don't install the skill. The last two came in round 3, as many as round 2, so the gate stopped; the owner chose to fix them and merge without a fourth round.
- Draws with mcp-excalidraw-server 2.1.2's offline renderer (Excalidraw's own exporter under jsdom), pinned in `runtime/excalidraw` and installed with `--ignore-scripts`. Chosen over the official Excalidraw MCP server, which shows diagrams inside a chat but doesn't document saving files, and over drawing with rough.js in HTML, which only imitates the look and can't be edited in Excalidraw. Reviewed before pinning: the package itself has no install scripts (its only dependency with one is fsevents, an optional macOS watcher the renderer doesn't use), and it reaches the network only for its `share` command (json.excalidraw.com), which the skill never calls.
