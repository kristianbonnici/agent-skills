## Deliberate qualifications

- The course's repository-only framing is a useful discoverability test, not a claim that agents cannot use approved connectors or ask people. Preserve the team's actual source of truth and expose necessary context through permitted access.
- Instruction length, feature counts, particular filenames, and rigid WIP rules are examples. Fit the current workflow; permit independent authorized work when another item is blocked.
- A passing state is not irreversible. Regressions and changed requirements invalidate current claims, while earlier results remain historical evidence.
- A successful process exit is insufficient when its assertions are vacuous. Validate gate behavior with known good and bad cases; do not copy illustrative shell expressions without checking their semantics.
- No test tier proves the absence of defects, and different tiers cover different failure classes. Select checks for actual boundaries and risk rather than assuming a strict containment hierarchy.
- Safe cleanup means respecting ownership and current work. Reverting local configuration, blanket deletion, or weakening merge checks does not follow from a readiness request.
- Independent reviewers and graphs can add value but do not guarantee correctness. A workflow may mix commands, agents, and human decisions. Persistent checkpoints still require safe handling of replayed side effects.
- Do not carry over unsupported percentages, dates, tool commands, or product capability claims. Primary sources and observable behavior take precedence; keep evaluation quality and cost separate.

The [source rationale](sources.md) records supporting primary sources and empirical counterweights. The skill's default remains audit-first, portable, adaptive, and on demand.
