# The bar

A source goes into an evidence file only when it is top tier, recent, and bears on a rule. When unsure, leave it out, with the reason under "Left out", so the owner can overrule it.

## Top tier

- **First-party reports** by AI labs and makers of coding agents about their own systems or studies, with a method or figures: an engineering or research post, a technical report. Not a launch announcement.
- **Peer-reviewed papers** in the main track of a major venue (NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, COLM, ICSE, FSE, ASE, ISSTA) or its datasets and benchmarks track.
- **Evaluation organizations' reports,** such as METR's and Epoch AI's.
- **Preprints from established groups:** several authors with named affiliations, a stated method over several models or a large set of tasks, and figures. The entry says "preprint"; a later run adds the venue, or marks it withdrawn.
- **Design sources,** for a rule adapted from someone's public code, skill or documentation: cited as where the idea came from, never as evidence that it works.

Not top tier:

- marketing, launch posts, and posts without a method or figures;
- newsletters, listicles, social media threads and podcasts;
- secondary coverage, except to read a source that refuses automated reading, named as such in the entry;
- preprints with a single author or without named affiliations;
- workshop papers;
- studies of a handful of runs, or of one person's use.

## Recent

Published or substantially revised within the last 24 months. An older source enters or stays only when it is still the standard reference on its point and nothing newer contradicts it; its entry says so. Results tied to particular models age faster: figures measured two or more model generations back, with nothing newer confirming them, are marked dated.

## Bears on a rule

It supports a rule of the skill with new figures, contradicts one, or shows a better one, and its entry names the rule after "→". A good source that bears on no rule is left out for that reason: interesting reading is the owner's digest's job, not the evidence's.
