# UX research

Run a fresh, source-grounded scan of user problems for the named digital product.

1. Restate the product, audience, time horizon, and scope.
2. Search current public sources such as forums, reviews, support communities, GitHub issues, Reddit, Hacker News, Stack Overflow, and relevant social or video discussions.
3. Use internal sources only when available and within the user's request.
4. Cluster evidence into product workflow, onboarding, docs/help, account/billing/permissions, reliability, and developer-experience problems.
5. Rank by severity, frequency signal, confidence, and product leverage.

Return:

- A 5–7 sentence executive read.
- Ranked problems with user goal, broken surface, evidence, severity, frequency signal, confidence, and recommended move.
- A source map that names weak or missing evidence.
- Opportunities grouped into this week, this quarter, and deeper research.

Separate observation from inference. Do not turn isolated anecdotes into prevalence claims or mix feature requests with workflow failures.
